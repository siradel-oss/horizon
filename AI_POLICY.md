# AI Policy

You are allowed to use any AI tools to assist in your contributions, as long as
you adhere to the following guidelines:

1. **Their use must be disclosed for non-trivial contributions.**
   Add a note in the commit message and/or pull request description, for example
   in the form of a `Co-authored-by:` or `Assisted-by:` line.

2. **You must review, verify, and understand any AI-generated content.**
   This implies being able to explain your contribution during review.
   We expect contributors to take responsibility for the quality and accuracy
   of their contributions, regardless of whether AI tools were used.
   [Contributor terms](CONTRIBUTING.md) also apply to AI-generated content.

3. **We do not want to talk to an AI.**
   We expect commit messages, pull requests, issues, and replies to be written by
   humans, for humans. Autonomous agents must not open issues or pull requests.
   You may include AI-generated text (diagnostics, reports, etc.), but it must
   be clearly labeled, e.g. included in a blockquote or a `<details>` section.
   Code comments and documentation may be AI-assisted, but should be clear to a
   human reader.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close issues and pull requests that don't follow this policy.

Repeated or deliberate violations, such as undisclosed AI use or AI-generated
content presented as human-written, fall under the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
("Misleading identity", "Failing to credit sources") and are handled through its
enforcement process.
