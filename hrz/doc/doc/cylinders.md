---
Title: Cylinders
Category: Vectors
---

A cylinder is extruded along the length of each polyline feature. See per-instance and per-layer properties here: [[CylinderVectorRepr]].

!!! note "Animation support"
    Cylinders can be animated using the `animation_speed` property. Be aware that the engine will have to redraw the entire scene at every update when any animated feature is visible on screen, which will have a strong impact on the device's power consumption.
