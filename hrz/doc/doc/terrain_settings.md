## Raster settings

Part of the [[SceneSettings]], [[RasterSettings]] dictate the appearance of the imagery rasters that are displayed on the terrain. These settings can affect how sharp the rasters appear, and what resolution they are downloaded at. See the documentation for more details.

## Terrain settings

The [[TerrainSettings]] from [[SceneViewSettings]] modify the appearance of the terrain in each view. The terrain can be made transparent, and its base color can be changed (by default it is blue). This is also where the clipping plane applied to the terrain can be chosen, and where the lighting of the terrain can be changed.
